## 2. Key Policy Rules

### 2.1 Surcharge (加收) vs Extension (扩展)

- **加收项**: Add-on to base item price. Must always pair with the base item.
- **扩展项**: Extends scope of the base item, same price as base. Independent billing allowed.

### 2.2 Recommended Treatment Packages (组套方案)

From training materials:

**Basic package**: 3 body-part massages + 1 acupuncture method (常规针法 or 特殊针具针法)

**Condition-based additions**: moxibustion (灸法) + cupping (拔罐)

**Stackable extras**: 耳穴疗法 + 穴位埋入 + 中药烫熨

### 2.3 Multi-Part Massage Rules

When prescribing multiple body-part massages:
1. Diagnosis must justify each body part
2. Treatment time must be sufficient for all parts
3. If treating one disease that involves multiple areas, charge only the primary treatment area

### 2.4 Children Definition

儿童 = 6 years old and under (6周岁及以下).
